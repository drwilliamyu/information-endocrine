Medicines for osteoporosis

This guide compares the two main types of bone medicine used in Australia. It covers how they work, how well they prevent fractures, their side effects, and how side effects are managed. A worksheet at the end helps you collect your own results before you talk with your specialist.

The short version

1 · The condition

Osteoporosis and osteopenia

Osteoporosis is a condition of thin, weak bones. Weak bones break more easily. A small fall can cause a fracture.

Osteopenia is bone density that is lower than normal. It is not as low as osteoporosis.

NormalT-score −1.0 or higher
OsteopeniaT-score between −1.0 and −2.5
OsteoporosisT-score −2.5 or lower, or a fracture from a small fall at any T-score
2 · The measurement

Bone density is the main measure, not the full picture

A DXA scan measures bone mineral density (BMD). BMD is the main measurement that doctors use today.

BMD shows only part of your fracture risk. Other factors also change the risk:

  • your age
  • fractures in the past
  • falls
  • some medicines and medical conditions
  • bone quality, which a scan cannot fully measure

Tools such as FRAX and the Garvan calculator combine these factors.

3 · The two types of medicine

Antiresorptive

Slow down bone loss

These medicines slow the cells that remove bone.

ClassMain example (trade name)
BisphosphonatesAlendronate (Fosamax)
Also risedronate (Actonel) and zoledronic acid (Aclasta)
RANKL inhibitorsDenosumab (Prolia)
Biosimilars: Jubbonti, Stoboclo, Ospomyv
Anabolic

Build new bone

These medicines make the cells that form bone more active. Doctors use them for very high fracture risk.

ClassMain example (trade name)
PTH-receptor agonistsTeriparatide (Forteo)
Also abaloparatide (Eladynos)
Sclerostin inhibitorsRomosozumab (Evenity)

After an anabolic course, you must start an antiresorptive. If you do not, you lose most of the new bone.

4 · Side effects to know about

Bisphosphonates

  • Tablets: heartburn. Take with a full glass of water and stay upright for 30 minutes.
  • Infusion: flu-like symptoms for 1–3 days after the first dose (about 1 in 3 people).

Denosumab

  • Do not stop or delay a dose. If you stop without a plan, spine fractures can occur.
  • Low calcium can occur, mainly with kidney disease.

Teriparatide and abaloparatide

  • Dizziness when you stand up. Inject sitting or lying down.
  • Nausea, and a fast heartbeat with abaloparatide.

Romosozumab

  • A small increase in heart attack and stroke.
  • Not for you if you had a heart attack or stroke in the past.

Rare risks for all antiresorptives and romosozumab

Jaw bone problem (ONJ) and unusual thigh-bone fracture (AFF). Both are rare. Tell your doctor about jaw pain, a dental problem that does not heal, or new thigh or groin pain.

1 · Bệnh

Loãng xương và thiếu xương

Loãng xương là tình trạng xương mỏng và yếu. Xương yếu dễ gãy hơn. Một cú ngã nhẹ có thể làm gãy xương.

Thiếu xương là khi mật độ xương thấp hơn bình thường. Mức này chưa thấp bằng loãng xương.

Bình thườngChỉ số T từ −1,0 trở lên
Thiếu xươngChỉ số T từ −1,0 đến −2,5
Loãng xươngChỉ số T từ −2,5 trở xuống, hoặc gãy xương do ngã nhẹ ở bất kỳ chỉ số T nào
2 · Cách đo

Mật độ xương là thước đo chính, nhưng chưa đủ

Máy chụp DXA đo mật độ khoáng xương (BMD). Hiện nay, BMD là thước đo chính mà bác sĩ dùng.

BMD chỉ cho biết một phần nguy cơ gãy xương. Các yếu tố khác cũng làm thay đổi nguy cơ:

  • tuổi của bạn
  • đã từng gãy xương
  • té ngã
  • một số thuốc và bệnh lý
  • chất lượng xương, mà máy chụp không đo được đầy đủ

Các công cụ như FRAX và Garvan kết hợp các yếu tố này.

4 · Tác dụng phụ cần biết

Bisphosphonate

  • Thuốc viên: ợ nóng. Uống với một ly nước đầy và ngồi hoặc đứng thẳng trong 30 phút.
  • Truyền tĩnh mạch: triệu chứng giống cúm trong 1–3 ngày sau liều đầu (khoảng 1 trong 3 người).

Denosumab

  • Không được ngừng hoặc trễ liều. Nếu ngừng mà không có kế hoạch, có thể gãy xương cột sống.
  • Canxi máu có thể thấp, chủ yếu ở người bệnh thận.

Teriparatide và abaloparatide

  • Chóng mặt khi đứng dậy. Hãy tiêm khi ngồi hoặc nằm.
  • Buồn nôn; tim đập nhanh với abaloparatide.

Romosozumab

  • Tăng nhẹ nguy cơ nhồi máu cơ tim và đột quỵ.
  • Không dùng nếu bạn đã từng bị nhồi máu cơ tim hoặc đột quỵ.

Nguy cơ hiếm gặp với mọi thuốc chống hủy xương và romosozumab

Hoại tử xương hàm (ONJ) và gãy xương đùi không điển hình (AFF). Cả hai đều hiếm. Hãy báo bác sĩ nếu đau hàm, vết thương răng miệng không lành, hoặc đau mới ở đùi hay háng.

1 · 疾病

骨质疏松症和骨量减少

骨质疏松症是指骨骼变薄、变弱。弱的骨骼更容易骨折。轻微跌倒就可能导致骨折。

骨量减少是指骨密度低于正常，但还没有低到骨质疏松症的程度。

正常T值 −1.0 或更高
骨量减少T值在 −1.0 至 −2.5 之间
骨质疏松症T值 −2.5 或更低，或在任何T值下因轻微跌倒而骨折
2 · 测量方法

骨密度是主要指标，但不是全部

DXA扫描测量骨矿物质密度（BMD）。BMD是目前医生使用的主要指标。

BMD只反映部分骨折风险。以下因素也会影响风险：

  • 年龄
  • 以前的骨折
  • 跌倒
  • 某些药物和疾病
  • 骨骼质量（扫描无法完全测量）

FRAX和Garvan等工具会综合这些因素。

4 · 需要了解的副作用

双膦酸盐类

  • 片剂：烧心。用一满杯水送服，服药后保持直立30分钟。
  • 静脉输注：首次用药后1–3天可能出现类似流感的症状（约每3人中有1人）。

地舒单抗

  • 不要停药或推迟用药。如果没有计划就停药，可能发生脊椎骨折。
  • 可能出现低血钙，主要见于肾病患者。

特立帕肽和阿巴洛肽

  • 站起时头晕。请在坐着或躺着时注射。
  • 恶心；阿巴洛肽可引起心跳加快。

罗莫佐单抗

  • 心脏病发作和中风的风险略有增加。
  • 如果您曾经发生过心脏病发作或中风，不适合使用。

所有抗骨吸收药物和罗莫佐单抗的罕见风险

颌骨坏死（ONJ）和非典型股骨骨折（AFF）。两者都很罕见。如果出现下颌疼痛、口腔伤口不愈合，或大腿、腹股沟出现新的疼痛，请告诉医生。

If you have osteopenia

  • Treatment depends on overall risk (age, FRAX or Garvan, falls), not the T-score alone.
  • If treatment is warranted, zoledronic acid has the best evidence.
  • Anabolics are not used unless a fracture has happened.
  • Lifestyle, vitamin D, calcium intake and falls prevention apply to everyone.

If you have osteoporosis

  • Every medicine above has proven fracture reduction.
  • At high risk, a bisphosphonate or denosumab is usual.
  • At very high risk (recent or multiple fractures, T-score ≤ −3.0), an anabolic first, then an antiresorptive, gives the most protection.

Your results

Understanding your numbers

A DXA scan (dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry) measures bone density, usually at the lower spine and hip. The report gives three numbers for each site.

BMD

Actual bone density

Measured in g/cm². It is the raw result. Values differ between scanner brands.

Best use: tracking change over time on the same scanner. A change of about 3–5% is needed to be real, not measurement noise.

T-score

Compared with young adults

How many standard deviations (SD) your density sits from a healthy 30-year-old of the same sex.

≥ −1.0
Normal
−1.0 to −2.5
Osteopenia
≤ −2.5
Osteoporosis
≤ −3.0
Severe range

Best use: diagnosis and PBS eligibility in postmenopausal women and men aged 50+. Your lowest site counts.

Z-score

Compared with your age group

How many SD you sit from people of your own age and sex.

A Z-score of −2.0 or lower means density is below the expected range for age. This prompts a search for an underlying cause.

Best use: premenopausal women, men under 50, and checking for secondary causes.

Reading your score as a percentage of people

Your score tells you what share of people sit above or below you

Bone density in a population follows a bell curve. Most people sit near the middle. A T-score or Z-score says how far from the middle you are. That converts directly into the percentage of people with higher or lower density than you.

T-score: compared with healthy 30-year-olds

A T-score of −2.5 means only about 1 in 160 healthy young adults have bone as thin as yours. Bone loss with age is normal, so this level becomes common later in life. It still marks a high fracture risk.

Z-score: compared with people your age and sex

A Z-score of −2.0 places you in the lowest 1 in 44 for your age. That is more loss than age explains, so we look for a cause. A Z-score near 0 means your bone is typical for your age, even when the T-score is low.

Fracture risk calculators

FRAX estimates your 10-year chance of a major osteoporotic fracture (spine, hip, wrist, shoulder) and of a hip fracture. It uses age, sex, BMI, prior fracture, parental hip fracture, smoking, alcohol, glucocorticoids, rheumatoid arthritis and hip BMD.

Common treatment thresholds are a major fracture risk of 20% or more or a hip fracture risk of 3% or more. The Garvan calculator is an Australian alternative that also counts falls.

Both tools underestimate risk with high-dose glucocorticoids, multiple or recent fractures, and frequent falls.

Blood tests before treatment

  • Vitamin D (25-OH vitamin D): aim for at least 50 nmol/L before starting. Many specialists aim for 75 before an infusion or denosumab.
  • Calcium (corrected or ionised): must be normal.
  • eGFR (estimated glomerular filtration rate): kidney function. Guides the choice of medicine.
  • PTH, ALP, thyroid, testosterone (men), coeliac screen: look for secondary causes.
  • Bone turnover markers (CTX, P1NP): show how fast bone is being removed and formed. Used to check response.

A copy of a biological medicine, approved by the TGA as working the same way.
